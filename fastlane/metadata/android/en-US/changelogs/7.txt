The app icon is fixed. It was squashed into a 2:3 rectangle at every
screen density, and Android 8 and newer had no adaptive icon to mask, so
it drew the old one shrunk onto a white badge. There is now a proper
square icon, and an adaptive one that keeps the stopwatch whole whatever
shape your launcher masks it to.

Nothing else changed. Same signing key, so it installs over 1.5.0 and
keeps your settings and history.
