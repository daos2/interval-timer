Interval Timer runs work-and-rest workouts — HIIT, Tabata, circuits,
stretching, breathing drills — so you can keep your eyes off the screen and
your hands free.

Set the work interval, rest, number of sets and tick frequency, then press
start. A countdown gives you time to get into position, and from there the app
talks to you in sound: a two-tone marker opens each work period and sounds
again when rest begins, a tick counts every unit, and the last unit of a period
ticks longer as a warning. The phone can stay in your pocket.

<b>Features</b>

* Four configurable fields, each with coarse and fine buttons. Frequency is
  held to whole hundredths of a second, so repeated taps never drift.
* Prestart countdown of 0 to 20 seconds; set it to 0 to start instantly.
* Large countdown with a progress ring, a set counter and an optional overall
  progress bar. The screen stays awake while a session runs.
* Six tick sounds and three completion fanfares, previewable before you choose.
  All are synthesised in memory, so the app ships no audio files.
* Statistics: a bar chart of training time by day, week or month that scrolls
  sideways and opens on the newest period. Rest days stay on the axis as empty
  bars. Tap a bar for its exact total, date range and session count.
* Weekly totals and a full session list showing date, settings, duration and
  whether the session was completed.
* Backup and restore: export your settings and history to a .zip and import
  them back. The archive is plain JSON, so you can read or edit it with any zip
  tool. Imports are validated in full and confirmed before anything is
  replaced, so a damaged or foreign file changes nothing.

<b>Privacy</b>

Everything is stored on your device. There is no account, no advertising and no
analytics, and the app requests <b>no Android permissions at all</b> — backup
files go through the system file picker, which needs none. It works entirely
offline.

<b>Languages</b>

English, German, French, Spanish, Russian and Georgian. Pick one in Settings,
or leave it following your phone. Dates, month names and plural forms follow
your choice. Languages are listed in their own language, so you can always find
your way back.

Translations other than English are machine-produced and have not yet been
reviewed by native speakers — corrections are very welcome.

<b>Recorded time is honest</b>

A session's recorded duration is actual trained time. It excludes the prestart
countdown and any time you spent paused.
